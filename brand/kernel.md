# Southern Fadez — Brand Kernel

Selected from the client concept (2026-09-02). Restored after a Line-Up pass.

## Positioning

A Prairieville barbershop that sells the chair. **Good cuts. Good company.** Established 2016. Founder Chaz (Darth Fadez). Crew: Chaz, Jay, Trey, Caleb.

## Audience

Men and families in Prairieville / Ascension Parish who want a fade that holds and a shop that still feels like a shop.

## Primary job of the site

Book a chair. Then prove the room and the people.

## Voice

Warm, direct, Southern without plantation nostalgia. Short sentences. No “look sharp.” No “vibes.”

## Visual tension

Cinematic shop photography × editorial type. Dark forest interiors against ivory ticket stock. Gold used as metal (clipper lever, chair hardware, the Z) — not luxury spray.

## Signature

The gold Z in Fadez. Script only for *Darth Fadez*. Forest / ivory split like a dark shop wall against a towel.

## Not

Sports. Merch. Racing. Crowns. Oxide industrial rails. Fake review counts. Photos of other shops’ logos.
