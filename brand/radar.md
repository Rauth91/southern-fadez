# Baton Rouge barbershop feature audit — September 2026

## What the local field does well

- Mercer Supply gives the shop a specific voice, explains policies, and treats each barber as a person rather than a card in a team grid: https://mercersupply.co/
- The Parker Barber explains what each service includes instead of publishing names and prices alone: https://parkerbarber.com/baton-rouge/
- Ultimate Fresh Cutz makes work, barbers, reviews, hours, and location easy to find in one pass: https://www.ultimatefreshcutz.com/
- Mr. Carter’s connects the physical room, products, hours, and a shop video: https://mrcartersbarbershop.com/
- Southern Fadez already has the strongest raw proof in its own TheCut profile: precise service descriptions, current pricing, and verified client history: https://search.thecut.co/barbers/SouthernFadez

## Pattern classification

- **Current:** individual barber profiles, transparent duration and price, a practical visit block, and real work photography.
- **Emerging locally:** matching a visitor to a barber by the cut they want, and showing useful live shop status without forcing a booking flow.
- **Saturated:** large generic galleries, four-card review grids, repeated booking buttons, “premium/luxury/elite” claims, and every section using the same title-copy-button formula.
- **AI cliché risk:** dark luxury palette plus giant serif copy plus gold rules when none of it connects to a real shop detail. Southern Fadez avoids this by tying the interface to Suite B, four specific chairs, actual services, and Airline Highway.

## What Southern Fadez borrows

- Mercer’s specificity about the chair and barber.
- Parker’s service-first usefulness.
- The local field’s easy-to-find hours and location.

## What Southern Fadez avoids

- Promotional calls to action, gift-card blocks, invented social proof, generic luxury language, and another gallery built from concept photography.

## Feature decision

1. Add a cut-to-barber selector inside the existing barber chapter instead of creating another full section.
2. Add a compact live shop board after the origin story with today’s regular hours, status, address, and phone.
3. Defer policies, reviews, and a work gallery until the owner supplies verified policy language and real Southern Fadez photography.
